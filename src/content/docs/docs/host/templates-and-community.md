You do not have to start from a blank quiz. Your instance has a catalogue of templates shared by its hosts, and it can also offer community quizzes published by other authors. This page covers taking a quiz from either, and sharing your own.

## Three ways to share, three different things

| | Who sees it | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Share with the instance’s other hosts** (quiz settings) | The other hosts of your instance, in their quiz list | Your quiz, read-only. **Create a quiz from this** gives them a copy. |
| **Share as a template** | Every host of your instance, in **Templates** | A copy in the catalogue, to take independent copies from. |
| **Export for publication** | Anyone, once you publish the file in the community store | A file checked against what the community store accepts. |

## Take a template

**Templates** opens **Shared templates**: the quizzes the hosts of this instance have shared, including the three sample quizzes QuizDock ships (**Discover France**, **Discover Taiwan**, **Discover Türkiye**).

1. Search, filter by language or tags, and sort (**Recently shared**, **Title (A–Z)**, **Most questions**).
2. Open a template to preview its questions and slides, its author and its licence.
3. Choose **Create a quiz from this**.

You get an independent copy in **My quizzes**, as a new draft with its own media: yours to rename, cut, publish and present. Nothing is pushed back to you afterwards: if the author shares a new version, your copy does not change.

## Share a quiz as a template

1. Mark the quiz ready (**Publish (ready)**).
2. In its settings, under **Language & sharing**, choose a **Licence** (CC0, CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0). A template is meant to be reused, so the licence is required: it travels with every copy. Set the **Language** and a few **Tags** too, so that other hosts find it.
3. In **More**, choose **Share as a template**, then confirm.

The editor confirms with "Shared as a template (revision 1)." Other hosts take their own copies and change them as they please; your quiz is never modified by what they do. Sharing again later replaces the catalogue entry with the new version and raises its revision.

To stop offering it, choose **Withdraw** on the template in **Templates**. Only its author or an administrator can withdraw a template. The copies already taken are not touched.

You must hold the rights to the quiz's content and media. Credit each media (author, licence, source) in its **Credit** field; see [Media](/docs/host/media/).

Sharing is off on the [live demo](https://quizdock-standalone.onrender.com).

## Community quizzes

When your instance is connected to a community catalogue, **Shared templates** shows a **Community quizzes** link. Whether it appears is an instance setting: ask whoever runs your instance. The community catalogue is still in preparation and may be empty.

On **Community quizzes**:

1. Search, filter by language and tags, and sort.
2. Open a quiz to preview its questions, slides and media. Each one shows its author, its licence and its **Source**.
3. Choose **Create a quiz from this**. The copy lands in your bank as a new draft and does not receive updates.

Quizzes come from other authors: check the source, the licence, the answers and the media credits before you play one. **Report a problem** opens the author's issue tracker with the quiz's details filled in.

## Publish to the community store

**Export for publication** (in **More**) checks the quiz against what the community store accepts, then downloads the file to put in your own quiz repository for the store. It lists:

- **The quiz is ready**;
- the **Language**, the **Licence** and the **Tags** (at least one);
- the **Size**, at most 20 MB by default;
- **{n} media without a credit**, or **Every media has a credit**.

Each check that fails says how to fix it. **Short name** is how the store knows the quiz in your repository; changing it later makes a new quiz for the store. The store's contributor guide describes how to publish the file.

## Questions

### Does someone who copied my template get my changes?

No. A copy is independent from the moment it is taken. Sharing again updates the catalogue entry, so later copies get the new version; earlier copies stay as they were.

### Can other hosts change a quiz I share with them?

No. A quiz shared with the instance's other hosts is read-only for them. They can only create their own copy.

### Can I share a quiz that uses photos from the web?

Only if you hold the rights to them. Pick media under a free licence (the media library links to free libraries when the instance offers them) and credit each one.
