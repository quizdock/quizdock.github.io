A selection is a named list of quizzes, in order, to present into a lobby in one go: after each quiz, the lobby picks the next one. This page covers making a selection, presenting it, saving a lobby as one, and sharing it.

## The selections

**Quizzes** has a **Selections** tab, next to **My quizzes**, **Templates** and **Themes**. It lists your selections and those other hosts share with you, as cards or a list. **Search the selections**, **Owner** (once another host shares one with you) and **Sort by** narrow and order it.

Each selection shows its first quizzes, its number of quizzes ("3 quizzes · 2 playable" when some cannot be played), and its owner when it is another host's. A selection you share carries **Shared**. Its main action is **Present**. On yours, **⋮** holds **Delete**: its quizzes and the results of its launches stay. On another host's, **Create a selection from this** sits next to **Present**.

## Make a selection

1. Choose **New selection**. It opens, named "New selection".
2. Edit its name in place, up to 60 characters, and a **Description** if you want one: what it is for, who it is for.
3. Choose **Add a quiz**: one of your quizzes, or one shared with you. Each quiz once.

A selection holds at most 50 quizzes. Changes are saved as you make them.

Under **Quizzes, in order**, each line shows the quiz's first step as a small picture, its title, its number of questions, its owner when it is another host's, its pace and **Autostart**. Select a line to set:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Pace | **Paced by hand** (the default): you move on yourself. **Automatic pace**: the lobby starts the quiz with **Autoplay** on. |
| **Autostart** | On (the default), the lobby's 30-second countdown starts the quiz, as for any next quiz in a lobby. Off, it waits for **Start the quiz**. |

To reorder, drag a line by its handle, or select it and use the arrows. A line's **⋮** offers **Open the quiz**, **Preview**, the moves, and **Remove from the selection**.

A quiz that cannot be played stays in the list, greyed, with why: "draft: cannot be played", "archived: cannot be played" or "no question: cannot be played". A deleted quiz leaves the list.

## Present a selection

Choose **Present**, on the selection or on its card. The lobby opens on the first quiz that can be played, with its pace. From there it is an ordinary lobby: same console, same choices, same archives. See [Start a lobby](/docs/host/start-a-room/).

After each quiz, going back to the lobby picks the selection's next quiz on its own: the first of the list the lobby has not played yet, with its pace and its **Autostart**. A quiz that cannot be played is skipped. **Change quiz** still picks any other quiz: the selection then goes on where it was, and a quiz you skipped stays next until it is played. Once every quiz is played, the lobby waits for **Choose the quiz**.

In the lobby, the console shows the selection, "Selection: {name} · 2 of 3": its quizzes that can be played, those played ticked, and the next one.

**Present** is unavailable when none of the selection's quizzes can be played.

## Save a lobby as a selection

A lobby you opened from a single quiz can become a selection once it has played a quiz. **Save as a selection** is offered:

- in the console's lobby, and when the lobby closes: every quiz the lobby played, in order, each with the pace it had;
- afterwards, on the lobby's card in **Sessions** and in a session's report: the lobby's archived quizzes, in order, each paced by hand. See [Reports and history](/docs/host/reports-and-history/#a-sessions-report).

The new selection is named after the lobby, and leaves out the quizzes you can no longer read. "Saved as a selection: open it" leads to it.

## Launches

The selection's page lists its **Launches**, newest first: each lobby you opened from it, with its date, its participants, and each quiz's success rate. A launch shows once its results are kept: a quiz closed without **Archive this quiz's results** is not in it. Another host's launch of your shared selection never shows here.

## Share a selection

Under **Sharing**, tick **Share with the instance’s other hosts**. They see it among their selections, read-only, and can:

- **Present** it: they play the quizzes they may read, and each one shared with them is played keeping nothing (see [Templates and community](/docs/host/templates-and-community/#present-a-quiz-another-host-shares));
- **Create a selection from this**: their own selection, on the same quizzes. It copies none of them, and nothing links the two afterwards.

Sharing a selection does not share its quizzes: you share each one in its own settings. **Sharing** says how many of its quizzes are not shared: the other hosts do not see them. A selection none of whose quizzes they may read does not show to them at all.

## Questions

### Does a selection keep a copy of its quizzes?

No. It refers to them: a quiz you edit between two launches is played in its new version. Each archived session keeps its own copy of the quiz as it was played.

### Can a quiz appear twice in a selection?

No, each quiz once. To play a quiz again in the lobby, pick it with **Change quiz**.

### Does a selection set the lobby's choices?

No. **Record all answers**, **Personalised tracking**, participant access, the language of the screens and the audio stay the lobby's, chosen at each launch. A selection only holds what depends on its quizzes: their order, their pace and **Autostart**.
